*Always under construction*

Place where I write/log stuff about my hobby, side projects etc.

* In the summer of 2026, I started learning pottery (handbuilding and wheel throwing) at [Mudville Clay Studio](https://www.mudvilleclaystudio.com/) - it is a lot of fun! I enjoyed taking classes, making art alongside several community artists (including kids in middle school!). Will be sharing gallery of my art pieces sometime soon.
* I volunteer at [Village Gallery of Arts](https://villagegalleryarts.org/) as a Gallery Assistant from time to time. I am an amateur 2D painter and consider painting as my way to connect with nature. I have been using mostly watercolor as medium but of late I have transitioned into using acrylic on canvas as primary choice. I feel art and science are complementary of each another - one needs the other to co-exist. Most of the interesting people I have met in my life have been artists, who practised arts in all forms!
* I love hiking! Some of my favourite trails in PNW are along the beautiful Columbia gorge and towards the Oregon coast. Recently, in summer of 2026, I climbed Mount St. Helens which was a great experience! Looking forward to learn more about alpine climbing and high altitude mountaineering in next few years.
