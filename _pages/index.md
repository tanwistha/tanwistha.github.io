I am an adjunct professor at the [Department of Computer Science](https://college.lclark.edu/departments/computer_science/) at [Lewis and Clark College](https://www.lclark.edu/). Prior to this, for more than ten years, I have been a R&D engineer, a data scientist and subsequently a technical product/program manager working at the intersection of research, product and engineering in semiconductor manufacturing industry. I specialized in zero-to-one productionization of AI platform solutions. I had the opportunity to work for [Intel Corporation](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/homepage.html) and [Synopsys](https://www.synopsys.com/), where I developed and deployed AI and Machine Learning solutions for semiconductor manufacturing processess and electronic design automation (EDA) software development processes, respectively.

I graduated with a PhD in [Computer Science from George Mason University](https://cs.gmu.edu/) with a research focus on relational network learning. I have enjoyed living in Portland, Oregon with my family since 2014, while exploring beautiful PNW outdoors. This web space is to share my research works, writings, teaching and other miscelleneous projects (both technical and non-technical) - if something catches your attention and you want to learn more, feel free to drop me a line! Thanks for stopping by.
